Gemini problems on scene 2:
1. First run: HTTP 429 (per-minute limit). Scene 1's 7 per-line meaning checks had just been sent. Scene 2 fell back to MOCK.
2. Retry: HTTP 503. It fell back to MOCK again.
3. Second retry: succeeded.

3 of the approved lines carry a MOCK meaning-check result. These are now marked `is_mock` in `quality_report.json`.

On the second retry, one line was refused by the voice. The cause was ஃ in "எஃப்", which is missing from the MMS vocabulary. The documented aytham approximation was added (ADR 0021 §4), and the scene was re-rendered.

## Translation (Claude's reading; [UNVERIFIED] until a Tamil-literate rater checks it)

No meaning errors were found in the 14 lines. The numbers (1, 2, 3, 20, 60%) and the negation ("don't have soap") were kept.

Minor issues:
- "rinse" was translated as கழுவி ("wash");
- one list mixes noun cases;
- "hand sanitizer" was transliterated (ஹேண்ட் சானிடைசர்);
- a San Francisco web address was kept for a Tamil Nadu audience without being flagged.

The numbers check marked the number *words* in the speech script as UNVERIFIABLE, as designed (ADR 0008), so a person must confirm them.
