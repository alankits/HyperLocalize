PROJECT NAME:
HyperLocalize — Context-Aware Identity-Preserving Video Localization

PROJECT PURPOSE:
HyperLocalize is a context-aware video localization platform designed to adapt video content for different target audiences while preserving the intended meaning and incorporating identity, consent, human review, regeneration, approval, and auditability into the localization workflow.

CORE IDEA:
HyperLocalize goes beyond simple text translation or basic video dubbing. The project treats localization as a broader process that considers language, meaning, visual/scene context, target audience, identity-related requirements, human review, and quality control.

CORE WORKFLOW:

Create Localization Project
→ Upload & Analyze Source Video
→ Context-Aware Localization
→ Generate Localized Preview
→ Human Review
→ Regenerate Scene/Output if Required
→ Approve
→ Export Localization Package

CORE CONCEPTS:
- Context-aware localization
- Identity
- Consent
- Human review
- Scene/output regeneration
- Approval
- Quality
- Audit
- Evidence

REAL-WORLD PURPOSE:
HyperLocalize is an information-access and localization layer. It can potentially help important video-based information reach people across language and cultural barriers.

Potential application domains include:
- Health
- Food
- Water
- Energy

The project should NOT be described as directly solving food production, water scarcity, energy generation, medical treatment, or other underlying physical problems.

Instead, it can help people access and understand critical information across language and cultural barriers.
